Good to know
  • The Pineapple needs a way to reach the server. Connect it to your Wi-Fi with client mode on wlan2 (Networking, WiFi Client Mode). Don't use the MK7AC for that: Sentinel needs it to listen on 5 GHz. A server on your own network works too, without Internet.
  • Messages hold your network names and the addresses of your access points, and they leave the house. Use your own ntfy server if you'd rather keep them home.
  • Alerts are sent while Sentinel is listening, within about 10 seconds. Nothing older than the moment you turn notifications on is sent.
